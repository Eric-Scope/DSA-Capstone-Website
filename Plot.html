Discovery of the Death Note

Light Yagami, a highly intelligent high school student, discovers a black notebook titled "Death Note" dropped by the shinigami Ryuk. Upon reading its instructions, Light learns that writing a person's name in the notebook while picturing their face will result in that person's death. After initial skepticism, Light experiments with the notebook and confirms its lethal power. He subsequently resolves to use the Death Note to eliminate criminals worldwide and establish himself as the god of a new world free from evil.

L's Counteroffensive

The sudden wave of criminal deaths attracts international attention, and the enigmatic detective known only as L takes on the case. L deduces that the killer, whom the public has named "Kira," is located in Japan's Kanto region and is likely a student. Through a carefully orchestrated television broadcast, L successfully provokes Kira into revealing his location, thus narrowing the investigation. Light realizes he is facing an intellectual adversary of exceptional caliber, and the two begin a complex psychological battle.

Joining the Task Force

Light's father, Soichiro Yagami, leads the Japanese task force investigating Kira. L suspects Light may be Kira and arranges to meet him under the pretense of forming a partnership. Light joins the investigation to monitor L's progress while simultaneously continuing his activities as Kira. The two develop a tense working relationship characterized by mutual suspicion, with each attempting to expose the other while maintaining the appearance of cooperation. L employs increasingly invasive surveillance methods, including wiretaps and cameras, to gather evidence against Light.

The Second Kira Emerges

Misa Amane, a popular model and actress, obtains a second Death Note and begins killing criminals to support the original Kira. Unlike Light, Misa possesses the Shinigami Eyes, which allow her to see a person's name and remaining lifespan merely by looking at their face. She seeks out Light and professes her devotion to him, having deduced his identity as Kira. Though initially viewing her as a liability due to her reckless behavior, Light recognizes the strategic value of her abilities and manipulates her affections to secure her loyalty as an accomplice.

Confinement and Memory Gambit

As L's suspicions intensify, both Light and Misa are detained and held in confinement. To absolve himself of suspicion, Light orchestrates an elaborate plan: he relinquishes ownership of his Death Note, causing him to lose all memories associated with it. During this period, the killings continue under a third party, Kyosuke Higuchi, whom Light had previously manipulated. Light, now genuinely unaware of his former identity as Kira, assists L in capturing Higuchi. Upon touching the Death Note again, Light's memories return, and he executes a carefully prepared scheme to eliminate L while avoiding suspicion.

L's Demise

Through meticulous planning involving his shinigami Rem, Light engineers a situation in which Rem is compelled to kill L in order to save Misa's life. The Death Note's rules stipulate that a shinigami who uses the notebook to extend a human's life will die. Rem writes L's name in her Death Note, resulting in L's death and her own disintegration. With his greatest adversary eliminated, Light assumes control of the investigation and positions himself to continue his vision of creating a new world order as Kira, now with significantly reduced opposition.

Near and Mello's Investigation

Following L's death, two individuals trained as his potential successors emerge to continue the pursuit of Kira. Near, who operates with calculated logic and leads an investigative team, and Mello, who employs more aggressive and unconventional methods, both seek to expose Light. Their divergent approaches create additional complications for Light, who must now contend with multiple adversaries operating independently. Mello's actions, including kidnapping and collaboration with criminal organizations, force Light to adapt his strategies while maintaining his facade as L's successor.

The Final Confrontation

Near ultimately deduces the mechanisms of the Death Note and Light's identity as Kira. He arranges a meeting at a warehouse where he systematically dismantles Light's elaborate deceptions by presenting irrefutable evidence of his guilt. When Light's carefully constructed plans collapse, he attempts a final desperate gambit but is thwarted by Near's precautions. Wounded and cornered, Light flees and experiences visions of his victims before dying from a heart attack induced by Ryuk, who writes Light's name in his Death Note. The series concludes with Light's death, the end of Kira's reign, and the world's return to its former state, though the legacy of his actions remains a subject of debate.
